# Community Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We aim to make participation in [PROJECT / COMMUNITY NAME] a respectful and constructive experience for everyone.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and considerate.
- Give feedback about ideas and work, not personal worth.
- Welcome questions from beginners.
- Explain disagreements clearly and professionally.
- Respect privacy and do not publish another person's personal information.
- Focus on what is best for the community.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, threats, insults, or discriminatory language.
- Deliberate intimidation or personal attacks.
- Sharing private information without permission.
- Spam or repeated disruptive behavior.

## Reporting

Report concerns privately to [MAINTAINER CONTACT OR REPORTING CHANNEL]. Include relevant links or context, but avoid spreading private information publicly.

Maintainers should review reports fairly and take proportionate action, which may include a warning, content removal, temporary restriction, or removal from the project.

## Scope

This code applies in project spaces and when someone is representing the project or community.

## Attribution

This is a lightweight starter policy. Adapt it to your community, publish a clear reporting contact, and review it before adopting it.
